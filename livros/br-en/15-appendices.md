# Appendices

This part is for consulting, not for reading. Each table points to the lesson that explains the topic, and the lesson is where you learn it; here you check it. Use it when a form escapes you in the middle of a sentence, or when a mistake keeps coming back.

### Imperfect, imperative, participle

Only four verbs are irregular in the imperfect.

| Verb | {{eu}}, {{você}}, {{ele}} | {{nós}} | {{vocês}}, {{eles}} |
|---|---|---|---|
| {{ser}} | {{era}} | {{éramos}} | {{eram}} |
| {{ter}} | {{tinha}} | {{tínhamos}} | {{tinham}} |
| {{vir}} | {{vinha}} | {{vínhamos}} | {{vinham}} |
| {{pôr}} | {{punha}} | {{púnhamos}} | {{punham}} |

{{Ir}} and {{ver}} are regular in the imperfect: {{ia}}, {{via}}.

**Irregular imperative ({{você}}):** {{seja}}, {{esteja}}, {{vá}}, {{dê}}, {{saiba}}.

**Irregular participles:** {{feito}} ({{fazer}}), {{dito}} ({{dizer}}), {{visto}} ({{ver}}), {{escrito}} ({{escrever}}), {{aberto}} ({{abrir}}), {{coberto}} ({{cobrir}}), {{posto}} ({{pôr}}), {{vindo}} ({{vir}}).

**Two participles:** {{aceitado}} / {{aceito}}, {{acendido}} / {{aceso}}, {{entregado}} / {{entregue}}, {{pagado}} / {{pago}}, {{ganhado}} / {{ganho}}, {{gastado}} / {{gasto}}. The short form goes with {{estar}} and {{ser}} (section 11.6).

**Future subjunctive and personal infinitive:** built from the {{eles}} form of the simple past, as in section 14.5.
